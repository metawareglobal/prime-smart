How to read this report — the honest fine print

  • Numbers are still small. 22 clicks and ~1,110 impressions is a modest base, so big percentages come off small starting points. We show the raw numbers everywhere so nothing looks inflated.
  • Positions are averages, and low-traffic terms wobble. Most terms get only a handful of impressions a month, so a single search can move a position by several spots. Treat per-keyword positions as a direction, not an exact rank. ("telescopic flag" above is a good example — its drop is almost certainly noise on ~5 impressions.)
  • One month is a short window. The gains line up with the work done, but a single 28-day period cannot prove cause and effect on its own. SEO compounds over months.
  • "New" means newly visible, not yet a traffic driver. Terms like flag printing dubai are appearing now but still sit deep on page 5–6; the clicks come as they climb.
