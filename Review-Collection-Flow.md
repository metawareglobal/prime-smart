# Review-Collection Flow — earn real star snippets (white hat)

**Goal:** Get genuine Google + on-site reviews so you (a) enter the Map Pack and (b) show ⭐ star snippets in search — the legitimate way, unlike Deluxe Printing's site-wide fake-count trick. Target: **30 Google reviews in 60 days, 50+ in 90 days.**

---

## Step 1 — Set up the ask

- Create/verify your **Google Business Profile** (Al Khabaisi, Dubai; category = Flag store).
- Get your **Google review short link**: GBP dashboard → "Ask for reviews" → copy link (looks like `g.page/r/XXXX/review`). Turn it into a QR code too.
- Turn on **product reviews** in WooCommerce (Settings → Products → enable reviews + star ratings). This is what generates on-site `AggregateRating` stars once reviews come in.

## Step 2 — Ask every customer, at the right moment

Ask **right after delivery / on collection**, when satisfaction is highest. Every order = one ask. Channels: WhatsApp (primary), email, and a QR card in the box.

## Step 3 — WhatsApp templates (copy/paste)

**A. Immediately after delivery**
> Hi [Name], thank you for your order with Prime Smart Flags! 🇦🇪 We'd love a quick Google review — it really helps our small team. It takes 20 seconds: [Google review link]. Thank you!

**B. Gentle follow-up (2 days later, if no review)**
> Hi [Name], hope the flags look great at your [event/office]! If you have a moment, a short Google review would mean a lot: [Google review link] 🙏

**C. After a happy reply / repeat client**
> So glad you're happy with the flags! Would you mind sharing that as a Google review? Here's the link: [Google review link]. We really appreciate it.

**D. Ask for a photo review (best for SEO + Map Pack)**
> If you can add a quick photo of the flags in place to your review, it helps other Dubai businesses see real results — thank you!

## Step 4 — On-site reviews (for product star snippets)

- After delivery, also send: *"You can review this exact product here: [product URL]#reviews"* — on-site product reviews are what let you legitimately add `aggregateRating` to Product schema and show stars in Google.
- Seed the first few by asking your most satisfied recent clients.

## Step 5 — Respond to every review

Reply to all reviews (positive and negative) within 24–48h — Google rewards active profiles, and it builds trust. Keep replies short, thank them, mention the flag type/Dubai naturally (light keyword signal).

## Step 6 — Turn on the stars

- **Google stars** appear automatically on your GBP as reviews accumulate.
- **Search star snippets:** once you have several genuine on-site reviews on a product/service page, add the true `aggregateRating` to that page's schema (see the pillar-page file). Only ever use **real** counts — faking it risks a Google manual action.

---

### Do / Don't
- ✅ Ask everyone, make it one-tap, respond to all, use real numbers.
- ❌ Never buy reviews, never gate reviews (only asking happy customers is against Google policy in some regions — ask everyone), never reuse one aggregate rating across all products (that's the competitor's risky trick).

### Simple weekly routine
Mon: message last week's delivered customers (template A/B). Wed: reply to any new reviews. Fri: post one photo update to GBP. Repeat.
