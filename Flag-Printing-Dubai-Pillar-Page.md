### H2: Frequently Asked Questions — Flag Printing Dubai

**How much does flag printing cost in Dubai?**
Custom flag prices in Dubai depend on the flag type, size, fabric and base. As a guide, sail and teardrop flags typically start around AED 270, table flags are lower, and large outdoor or premium options cost more. Send your requirement on WhatsApp for an exact quote within minutes.

**How long does flag printing take in Dubai?**
Most custom flag orders are produced within a few working days once artwork is approved. Share your deadline when you request a quote and we'll confirm the turnaround, including options for urgent event timelines.

**What is the best flag material for the UAE climate?**
For outdoor flags exposed to sun, heat and wind, UV-resistant knitted polyester printed with dye-sublimation holds colour best. For double-sided visibility choose satin glossy; for premium indoor flags choose blockout super-matt.

**What's the difference between a sail flag and a teardrop flag?**
Both are outdoor advertising flags on a curved pole. Sail (curve-top) flags have a tall straight edge ideal for storefronts and entrances; teardrop flags have a rounded shape that holds its form well in wind, popular at exhibition stands. Both come in multiple sizes and bases.

**Do you print double-sided flags?**
Yes. Double-sided flags use a blockout liner between two printed panels so the design reads correctly on both sides with no bleed-through. Single-sided (mirror-image reverse) is also available.

**Which flags work best for events in Dubai?**
For outdoor events and exhibitions, sail, teardrop and L-shape flags give the most visibility. For conferences and boardrooms, table and conference flags are standard. For National Day and festivals, car flags, bunting and hoisting flags are most in demand.

**Do you deliver across the UAE?**
Yes — we deliver completed flag orders across Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah, and can coordinate delivery to specific event venues or offices.

**Can you design the flag if I only have a logo?**
Yes. Send your logo on WhatsApp and our team prepares print-ready artwork at no design charge before production.
