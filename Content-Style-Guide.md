# Content Style Guide — PrimeSmart.ae (applies to ALL site content)

> Governs every product description, category/landing page, blog post, meta description, and FAQ written during the 60-day plan. Client instruction, 17 Aug 2026.

## Hard rules
1. **No em-dashes (—) anywhere.** Also avoid en-dashes in ranges. Use commas, periods, "to", or parentheses instead. Example: write "AED 270 to 625", not "AED 270–625".
2. **Plain, everyday English.** No heavy or ornate vocabulary. If a simple word works, use it. Avoid words like "elevate, unparalleled, meticulous, seamless, bespoke, plethora, leverage, robust, cutting-edge, in the realm of."
3. **Human, formal-but-warm tone.** Write like a real person at a Dubai flag company talking to a customer. Professional and polite, not stiff, not salesy, not chatty.
4. **Read as human, not AI.** No detector can be guaranteed, so we write naturally instead of gaming a score (see "AI-tells to avoid").

## AI-tells to avoid (these are what make text read as machine-written)
- Em-dashes and semicolons stacked for rhythm.
- Every sentence the same length. Vary them: some short. Some a bit longer with a natural clause.
- Opening with "In today's fast-paced world / When it comes to / Whether you are."
- Tricolons everywhere ("durable, versatile, and eye-catching"). Use them rarely.
- "Not only... but also", "It's worth noting that", "Rest assured", "Look no further".
- Over-hedging ("can help to potentially").
- Perfectly balanced listicles with identical phrasing per item.
- Empty hype adjectives with no fact behind them.

## Do instead
- Lead with a concrete fact or a real use case.
- Give specifics: sizes, fabric, base options, turnaround, where it suits.
- Mention Dubai / UAE conditions naturally where true (heat, sun, wind, events).
- One clear call to action: WhatsApp for a quote and free artwork.
- Contractions are fine (we'll, you'll) since they read human, but keep it professional.
- British spelling to match UAE market (colour, organise) is fine and consistent.

## Meta descriptions
- 150 to 160 characters, plain, one benefit + location + action. No em-dash.

## Before/after example (sail flags)
**Before (AI-ish, has em-dash):** "Stand out with our premium Sail Flags—a dynamic and versatile branding solution designed to draw attention, crafted from durable, weather-resistant fabric."
**After (human, plain, no em-dash):** "Sail flags are one of the easiest ways to get noticed at an event or outside your shop. The curved top holds its shape and stays upright even on a calm day. We print them on weather-resistant fabric that copes well with the Dubai sun, so the colours stay bright through long outdoor use."
